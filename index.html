FACILITY ZONES

Key Tactical Sectors

Understanding the unique two-story layout of Nuke is vital for rotators, execute plays, and cross-map sound awareness.

LEVEL 1 - UPPER

A Site (Reactor Top)

Fast-paced, close-quarters site dominated by Hut, Squeaky, Main, and Heaven. High verticality with the iconic A Hut and Rafters overhead.

Key Angles: Rafters, Tetris, Hut Fast Execute
LEVEL 0 - LOWER

B Site (Sub-Level)

Deep subterranean site reached via Vents, Ramp, or Secret. Features the central Reactor Core, Dark, Toxic room, and Window control.

Key Angles: Dark, Vents, Control Hold-heavy
EXTERIOR

Outside / Yard

Long sightline corridor connecting T Spawn to Secret and Main. Requires precise smoke walls to cross safely against AWPs on Garage or Red Container.

Key Angles: Garage, Secret, Silo AWP Territory
ROTATION ROUTE

Secret & Tunnels

Stairwell transition leading directly from Outside into lower B Site. The ultimate strategic flank vector for Terrorist executes on B.

Key Angles: Stairwell, Lower Doors Flank Route
CHOKEPOINT

Ramp & Trophy

Steep incline leading from T-Lobby to B Site. High risk, high reward area heavily contested by CT holding with AWPs or XM shotguns.

Key Angles: Headshot, Ramp Box Close Combat
VERTICAL CONNECTORS

Vents & Heaven

Direct drop-down shafts and high elevated walkways enabling CTs to instantly rotate between A Site upper and B Site lower in seconds.

Key Angles: Heaven Ladder, Vent Drop Fast Rotate

COMPETITIVE METAGAME

Tactical Strategy Guide

Master side-specific dynamics and economy management on CS2's most vertical map.

CT Side Defense Blueprint

Rotation Speed & Vertical Holds

  • Outside Control is Essential: Holding Garage with an AWP limits Terrorist freedom and prevents unannounced Secret rotations down to B.
  • Vent Rotations: CTs can instantly descend through A site vents to reinforce B site within 3 seconds of a hit.
  • 3-1-1 Default Setup: 3 players covering A/Heaven/Rafters, 1 holding Outside, and 1 solo anchor holding Ramp.

T Side Attack Playbook

Pacing & Utility Overload

  • Outside Smoke Wall: Deploy 2-3 smokes across Outside to blind Garage and allow a stealth run down to Secret.
  • Squeaky & Hut Split: Time dual entries through Squeaky door and A Hut simultaneously to overwhelm A site defenders.
  • Conditioning CTs: Fake outside smoke walls early in the round to draw CT rotations away from Ramp or A Site.
